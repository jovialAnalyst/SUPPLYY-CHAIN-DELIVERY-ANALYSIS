# IronCore Fitness – Digital Transformation Consulting Project

## Overview
A comprehensive, semester-long consulting engagement for IronCore Fitness, 
a fitness brand navigating the shift to digital-first operations. This project 
was developed as a group consulting capstone at the University of Houston's 
Bauer College of Business, simulating a real-world strategy engagement from 
discovery through executive presentation.

## Business Problem
IronCore Fitness faced growing competitive pressure from digital fitness 
platforms and needed a structured roadmap to modernize its member experience, 
streamline operations, and build long-term digital capabilities — without 
disrupting existing revenue streams.

## Our Approach
We approached this as a full consulting engagement, delivering across multiple 
workstreams over the semester:

- **Discovery & Vision** – Defined the transformation vision and strategic 
  priorities aligned to IronCore's brand and growth goals
- **Roadmap Design** – Built a 3-phase digital transformation roadmap 
  sequencing quick wins, core platform builds, and long-term capability growth
- **Business Case** – Developed a financial model justifying the investment 
  with ROI, NPV, and payback analysis
- **KPI Framework** – Designed measurable success metrics across member 
  engagement, operational efficiency, and revenue performance
- **Agile Planning** – Translated strategy into actionable Agile user stories 
  for implementation teams
- **Executive Presentation** – Delivered a final "Why This Works" executive 
  deck to close the engagement

## Transformation Roadmap
| Phase | Focus | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Quick Wins | Immediate member-facing digital improvements |
| Phase 2 | Core Platform | CRM, app, and data infrastructure build-out |
| Phase 3 | Scale & Optimize | Analytics, personalization, and growth enablement |
